https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/POKEY

The sound chip used in the Atari 800 is the POKEY (Potentiometer and Keyboard Integrated Circuit), a custom 40-pin digital I/O and audio chip designed by Doug Neubauer. Released in 1979, it also manages keyboard scanning, serial I/O (the SIO bus), and potentiometer inputs like paddles.
